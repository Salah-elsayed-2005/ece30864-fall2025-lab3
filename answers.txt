a) Pros : Fast delivery of working software.  Continious development after the feedback.
   Cons : No long term planning.  Difficult to scale for large projects as subteams will get larger. Dependent alot on customer feedback.
b) Scrum master : Coach and leads the team. Ensure smooth collaboration and integration.
   Product owner: Define product and goals. Gives feedback on the delivered software. Represents stakeholder & customers. 
c) Sprint Planning : Discuss what work will be done in the sprint. Explain how they will achieve it.
   Sprint review   : Demonstrate and show work done. Get stakeholder feedback.
   Sprint Retrospective: See what went well. Discuss what can be improved next sprint. 
d) Epic : Large body of work broken into smaller stories.
   Story: A feature to be delivered to the end-user.
   Bug  : An error in the product that needs fixing that may block a story.
   Task : Represents a unit of technical or non-functional work.
